## 🚀 HOW TO USE

### Quick Upload (Fastest):
```
1. Go to http://localhost:5173/add-vehicle
2. Stay on "Quick Upload" mode (default)
3. Click upload area or drag files
4. Select 5-10 images at once
5. Click "Validate Image Quality"
6. Click "Run Damage Detection"
7. Review results and submit!
```

### Guided Upload (Most Organized):
```
1. Go to http://localhost:5173/add-vehicle
2. Click "Guided Upload" mode
3. Upload Front View image
4. Upload Rear View image
5. Upload Left Side image
6. Upload Right Side image
7. Upload Additional Damage image
8. Click "Validate Image Quality"
9. Click "Run Damage Detection"
10. Review results and submit!
```
